# Design Voice Within Dazzler's Existing Framework

October 7, 2026. Version 0.27.0, local development. No publication.

## Result

The creative standard now calls for the strongest coherent prompt-specific voice in every output. A basic professional request limits the available vocabulary, not the ambition. Generic or incoherent output needs revision; a committed design may divide taste without intentionally frustrating the user.

This is integrated into existing art direction, design principles, automatic workflow, document/web design guidance, refinement controls, workflow evidence and final artifact gates. There is no new theme system, default visual skin, separate exporter, or compulsory maximum setting. The existing font catalog and typography system, color engine, composition tools, visualization helpers, native document paths and design records carry the decisions.

## Behavioral Enforcement

Every workflow kind and scope includes distinction. A reported pass requires an observed review describing voice, prompt fit, defining move, supporting choices, restraint and a distinctive verdict, plus render evidence references. Missing reviews and declared generic/incoherent passes are rejected. Failed reviews remain findings; unavailable checks remain incomplete. Read-only audits remain read-only, and small changes preserve their scope and established voice.

The evidence helper checks the completeness and consistency of reported observations. It cannot authenticate a screenshot, measure taste or guarantee a reaction. The skill instructs the agent to render, inspect and revise; that instruction is not a mechanical proof that every invocation will comply.

## Evaluation Needed for a Stronger Claim

Use repeated matched prompts with unchanged content, assets and constraints across versions. Include a basic professional memo, a dense data report, a brand-locked interface, a reduced-motion experience and an open creative brief. Hide tool/version labels. Ask reviewers separately about memorability, prompt fit, clarity, preference and task completion. Record both generic-output and rejection rates. A visually surprising result that obscures facts or prevents the task is a failure. Do not replace human judgments with token counts, feature counts or screenshot differences.

## Research

- [The Public Theater, Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-public-theater): content-responsive typographic identity, with variation across productions. The transferable principle is a visual language that evolves with content.
- [MIT Media Lab, Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab): shared organizing rules support distinct related identities. The transferable principle is coherent variation.
- [Lusion](https://lusion.co/): live homepage visually inspected; its large three-dimensional scene works with a spare framing interface. The transferable principle is a dominant experience supported by restraint. Full interaction/accessibility behavior was not audited.
- [Google's expressive-design research](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research): the authors report benefits from expressive hierarchy and also failures when basic interaction cues are removed. Their research supports experimentation with clear task cues, not a universal claim that users enjoy being forced to adapt.

No source artwork, commercial assets or implementation were copied. These observations inform Dazzler's existing framework; they do not establish Dazzler's comparative effectiveness.
