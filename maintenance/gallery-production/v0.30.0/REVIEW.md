# Release 0.30 Gallery Review

Thirty newly authored examples were reviewed against the 0.29 collection: ten editable Word documents, ten HTML documents and ten interfaces. Existing fictional facts and local demonstration handlers were retained; composition, typography, surface roles and content sequence were reconsidered from the recorded briefs.

All ten Word files rendered as one page each in LibreOffice. Complete pages and embedded PDF font names were inspected. Landscape body measures were shortened and the technical verification column widened after review. Referenced Word fonts are not redistributed; another host or later edits can change pagination.

All twenty browser examples passed heading-case, axe WCAG A/AA, JavaScript-error and page-overflow checks at 1440 and 390 pixels. Applicable controls were exercised, including empty searches, restoring filters, clearing an empty order twice and completed board actions. Review also restored café price qualifications and filters, fine-dining service details, and removed internal composition rationale from application copy.

Final regression checks confirm synchronized embedded UI data, themeable color bindings and preservation of every native record value. Data tables retain repeated headers; the legal and family record-card layouts are checked for complete content instead of adding misleading table headers. The school web edition includes a zero-baseline median chart with mobile-sized labels and its exact source table.

The final evidence manifest binds reviewed previews to shipped source and runtime hashes. Shortlists are recorded separately; these designs retain their independently authored directions and are not presented as newly tested recipe winners. No model inference or private data was used. Visual judgments are agent observations, not a measured aesthetic outcome.
