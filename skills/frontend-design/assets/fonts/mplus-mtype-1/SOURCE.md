# M+ M Type-1 distribution

Checked: 2026-09-28

License: OFL-1.1

M+ 1m is the legacy M Type-1 family. Do not confuse it with newer proportional M PLUS 1/2 discussed in the site's description. Files come from a labeled legacy mirror; Japanese glyphs increase payload size.

Font binaries are copied byte-for-byte; they have not been subsetted, renamed internally, converted, or relicensed.
