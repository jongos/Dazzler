# Liberation Sans distribution

Checked: 2026-09-28

License: OFL-1.1

Liberation Sans targets Arial metrics. Times New Roman and Courier compatibility belong to other Liberation families, not this one.

Font binaries are copied byte-for-byte; they have not been subsetted, renamed internally, converted, or relicensed.
