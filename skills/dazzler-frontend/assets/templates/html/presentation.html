CIVIC LAB / THREE MINUTE BRIEF / The Speaker Brief

Library After Hours Pilot

Test one late opening each week before committing to a permanent change.

6 WEEKS

a bounded pilot with a clear review point

This fictional presentation handout proposes Thursday openings until 21:00. Attendance, staffing and quiet-study demand will determine the next decision.

01 / presentation

The Need

Evening visitors need access after work and classes. The pilot tests demand rather than assuming every weekday should open later.

02 / presentation

The Experiment

Open on six Thursdays. Keep a quiet study zone, a staffed help point and a clear closing routine. Record occupancy every hour.

03 / presentation

The Decision

Continue only if demand is sustained and the staffing plan is workable. Publish the measured results, including low-attendance evenings.
