How light affects seedling growth

Science investigation • Year 8

Student: Avery Kim Class: Year 7 Science Teacher: Jo Lee Date: 21 June 2027

Question and prediction

How does daily light exposure affect bean seedling height over two weeks? I predict that the group receiving more light will show healthier leaf growth. Height alone may not capture plant health.

Variables

TypeWhat is measured or controlled
IndependentDaily light exposure: 4 hours or 8 hours
DependentSeedling height in centimeters; leaf appearance
ControlledSeed type, soil, container, water and measurement time

Materials and method

  • Prepare six similar seedlings in identical containers; label three A and three B.
  • Place group A in 4 hours of light and group B in 8 hours. Keep other conditions consistent.
  • Water each container with the same measured amount daily.
  • Measure height from soil level at the same time on days 0, 7 and 14. Record observations.

Care and safety

Ask a teacher before using lamps. Keep water away from electrical equipment. Wash hands after handling soil.

What the example suggests

Group A grew from 2.1 to 7.2 cm on average. Group B grew from 2.1 to 8.2 cm. The difference in growth is 1.0 cm. With only three plants per group, this does not establish a general rule.
